01 — Project Overview

A 4,200 sq.ft workplace, finished in sixteen weeks.

FocusOn Interior Decorators Pvt. Ltd. delivered the complete interior and civil fit-out of a 4,200 sq.ft workplace for BRC Group in Kolkata. Working with Node Urban Lab as Project Management Consultant, the team mobilised on 1 May 2026 and handed over a finished, client-ready office on 18 August 2026 — sixteen weeks, on schedule.

Quality of workmanship was commended by CMD Mr. Arun Kumar Rai, who indicated interest in future collaborations.
